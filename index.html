Payroll Questions

Why should I outsource payroll?

Outsourcing saves the hours spent on calculations, filings and corrections, often costs less than in-house payroll staff and software, and moves day-to-day compliance work to specialists who handle it every day.

Can Key HR pay employees in multiple states?

Yes. We handle federal, state and local payroll tax rules, registrations and withholdings for employees in all 50 states and Puerto Rico, including remote employees.

What are FICA, FUTA and SUTA?

FICA covers Social Security and Medicare taxes shared by employers and employees. FUTA is the federal unemployment tax paid by employers. SUTA is the state unemployment tax, with rates and wage bases set by each state. Key HR calculates, withholds and deposits all three.

Can you pay independent contractors?

Yes. We help you collect a Form W-9, pay contractors on the terms you agree to and issue Form 1099-NEC to contractors who meet the IRS reporting threshold.

Can I run bonus or off-cycle checks?

Yes. Bonuses, commissions, corrections and final paychecks can be processed outside your regular payroll schedule.

How do employees get their W-2s?

Employees can view and download W-2s and pay stubs anytime in the employee portal. Our W-2 retrieval guide walks them through registration and access.

What other services can I add?

Compliance Questions

What does PEO compliance actually cover?

It spans four areas: federal payroll taxes, state tax registration and reporting, employment law and insurance and benefits requirements. Key HR takes on specific duties in each area and supports you on the rest, as outlined in your client service agreement.

Does a PEO take on all of my compliance responsibility?

No. A PEO shares employer responsibilities with you through co-employment. Key HR manages areas such as payroll tax filings, workers’ compensation and benefits administration, while your business remains responsible for daily supervision, workplace conduct and the decisions you make as an employer.

What is a Certified PEO (CPEO)?

The IRS Certified Professional Employer Organization program allows qualifying PEOs to assume sole liability for federal employment taxes on the wages they pay to worksite employees. Key HR is ESAC accredited, an independent standard for financial strength and ethical practices. Your Key HR team can explain exactly how federal tax responsibility is handled under your agreement.

What happens when I hire in a new state?

Let us know before the employee starts. Key HR sets up the required state tax accounts, confirms workers’ compensation coverage and flags the state’s wage, leave and posting requirements so you are compliant from day one.

Who files ACA reporting for my employees?

For employees enrolled in Key HR’s benefit plans, Key HR prepares and files Forms 1094-C and 1095-C. We work with you to collect the hours and eligibility information the reports require.

Do I still need my own attorney or CPA?

Yes, for matters outside the PEO relationship, such as legal representation, business income tax and other types of insurance. Key HR works alongside your advisors and keeps them informed when it helps.

What other services can I add?

Compliance is one part of a complete solution. Key HR also provides payroll, HR, risk management and employee benefits, all managed by one accountable team.

PEO Questions

Straight Answers About Working With a PEO

How do I choose the right PEO?

Look for independent accreditation such as ESAC, experience in your industry, a named service team rather than a call center, and pricing that clearly shows what’s included. Ask how multi-state compliance is handled and what support looks like after you sign.

Will my employees notice a change?

Mostly in good ways. They gain access to stronger benefits and a self-service portal for pay stubs, time off and tax forms. Paychecks keep arriving on schedule, and you remain their manager.

Who handles hiring and firing?

You do. Who you hire, how you manage and when you part ways all stay your decision. Key HR provides the guidance and paperwork so each decision is documented and compliant.

How is a PEO different from an employer of record?

An employer of record usually takes on the full legal employer role, often for workers in places where you have no business entity. A PEO shares employer responsibilities with your business through co-employment, and you keep control of daily operations.

How is a PEO different from an ASO?

An administrative services organization handles HR paperwork, but your business stays the only employer and keeps its own benefits and workers’ comp policies. A PEO adds access to group benefits and workers’ comp through co-employment.

Should I use a PEO or build an in-house HR team?

Many growing businesses find a PEO delivers more expertise for less than hiring several HR specialists. Companies that already have HR staff often use a PEO to free that team for strategic work.

What happens to benefits if we leave?

We plan the transition with you so employees keep coverage through the changeover, including continuation options where they apply.

What size business is a PEO best for?

PEOs fit growing businesses especially well. Key HR focuses on small to mid-size businesses across all 50 states and Puerto Rico.

What is a PEO? Learn how it works
What is a PEO?

A professional employer organization takes on payroll and payroll taxes, HR administration, compliance, workers’ comp and benefits for your business through co-employment. You keep running your team. Key HR handles the paperwork and compliance behind it.

Will I lose control of my business or my employees?

No. You still hire, manage, schedule and set pay, and every business decision stays yours. Key HR becomes the employer of record only for payroll, taxes, workers’ comp and benefits.

How is a PEO different from a payroll company?

A payroll company runs payroll. A PEO also brings HR support, compliance, workers’ comp and benefits under one service, and shares the employer responsibilities with you.

How does workers’ comp work through Key HR?

Coverage is built into your service, and premiums are paid as you go with each payroll, so there’s no large deposit up front. Higher-risk classes may be placed through our in-house insurance agency.

Why does ESAC accreditation matter?

ESAC independently checks that a PEO is financially sound and that payroll taxes, benefits and workers’ comp premiums are actually paid. It’s one of the strongest assurances a PEO can offer.
